Frequently Asked Questions

How can the Standard Advisory tier be 100% free for borrowers?

Lending institutions compensate FlexiLoan for pre-verifying borrower files. This referral commission is regulated and does NOT increase your loan interest rate or add markup charges.

Is the Premium ₹1,999 advisory fee refundable?

Yes! If our advisory desk fails to secure a lower interest rate offer than your existing lender bank quote, your ₹1,999 fee is 100% refunded immediately.

How long does a rate match negotiation take?

Standard rate comparisons take under 2 minutes digitally. Premium rate negotiations with partner bank underwriting desks are typically completed within 24 to 48 hours.
