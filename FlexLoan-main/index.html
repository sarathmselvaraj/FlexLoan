Frequently Asked Borrower Questions

Will checking my loan interest rates impact my credit score?

No, absolutely not. Performing an initial rate search or EMI calculation via FlexiLoan generates a soft credit inquiry only. This will never affect your credit score or credit rating history.

What basic documents are required to initiate a loan application?

Generally, you will need a valid government photo ID (Passport/Driver's License), proof of address, recent 3-month bank statements, and proof of income (paystubs or tax returns).

Can I prepay or foreclose my loan before the tenure completes?

Yes! Most of our partner floating-rate home loans carry 0% foreclosure or prepayment penalties. Personal and business loans allow flexible part-prepayments after 6 months.

How quickly will the approved loan amount reach my bank account?

Upon document verification and final sanction agreement sign-off, personal and business loans are disbursed within 24 hours. Home loans typically complete disbursement in 3-5 business days.
