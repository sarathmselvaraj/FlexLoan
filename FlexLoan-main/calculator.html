How Equated Monthly Installment (EMI) Is Calculated

EMI is calculated using the standard reducing-balance loan formula: EMI = [P x R x (1+R)^N] / [(1+R)^N - 1], where P is Principal amount, R is monthly interest rate, and N is loan tenure in months.

P = Loan Principal

Total capital borrowed from lender.

R = Monthly Rate

Annual rate divided by 12 months.

N = Tenure Months

Total monthly installments.

Calculator FAQs

Why does the interest component decrease each month? +
Because interest is calculated on the reducing principal balance. As you pay off principal every month, the outstanding debt shrinks, resulting in a lower interest charge for the next cycle.
What happens if I make a partial lump-sum prepayment? +
Partial prepayments directly reduce your principal balance. You can choose to either shorten your loan tenure (keeping monthly EMI same) or reduce your monthly EMI amount.
